Healthcare Systems • Relational PostgreSQL

ApexCare Clinical Hospital Management System

3NF Architecture

Architected a 3NF normalized clinical relational schema modeling 4 primary business entities: Departments, Attending Physicians, Master Patient Index, and Active Inpatient Admissions. Includes real-time bed occupancy telemetry and transactional validation.

Interactive SQL Sandbox
SELECT dept_name, count(room_code) as occupied, round((count(room_code)::numeric / total_beds)*100, 1) as occupancy_rate FROM admissions GROUP BY dept_name;
> Telemetry Result: 164 occupied / 200 total beds (82.4% overall hospital occupancy).
> Alert: ICU at 91.6% capacity (Surge protocols recommended).
